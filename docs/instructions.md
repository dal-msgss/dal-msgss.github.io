# New Executive, First Steps

The first thing you have to do is ask the former executive to (1) add you to the MSGSS team on Microsoft Teams and (2) transfer ownership of the MSGSS [Github organization](https://github.com/dal-msgss) (where this website is hosted, see [Maintaining this website](https://dal-msgss.github.io/docs/website.html)). Further credentials, including access to `msgss@dal.ca`, is on Teams.

Announcements to the student body can be made `mathgrad-mathstat@LISTS.DAL.CA` and `statgrad-mathstat@lists.dal.ca`.

#### President

* Login to `msgss@dal.ca` on the Outlook app on your PC or phone.
* Send a message to `dsusocieties@dal.ca` with the new execitove team.
* Send a message to `dsuvpi@dal.ca` requesting a "bank letter" (include the names of the President and VPs). Once the letter is received, book an appointment at the [RBC branch at Dalplex](https://maps.rbcroyalbank.com/NS-HALIFAX-branch-3314) to transfer signing authorities and gain access to mobile banking. Bring the letter, yourself, and the VPs to the appoinment.
* Coordinate a communications channel (on Discord or SMS, Whatsapp, etc.) with the new executive team.
* Complete the DSU [PEO training](https://www.dsu.ca/trainings-certification).
* Complete the DSU [EDIA training](https://www.dsu.ca/trainings-certification).
* Ask the former executive team about the Dalhousie Graduate Student Network (DGSN).

#### VP Internal (treasurer)

* Complete the DSU [treasurer training](https://forms.cloud.microsoft/pages/responsepage.aspx?id=mRm4YH8LLUGSo-F9iunj4Ajp1vuSmnVLoi7ROrfXqu5UN1cwVzVQSFdRMTNZS09NRTBRSTkzOEZSRi4u&route=shorturl).
* Set up mobile banking on your phone.
* Get acquainted with updating the website on Github.

#### VP External (communications & events)

* Complete the DSU [PEO training](https://www.dsu.ca/trainings-certification).
* Login to `msgss@dal.ca` on the Outlook app on your PC and phone.

#### Representatives (grad seminar)

* Send a message to  `mathstat@dal.ca` booking a regular time for the seminar at Chase 319.
* Distribute the responsibility of communications and email updates about the grad seminar.
* Elect a representative to take on chairing the seminar itself.

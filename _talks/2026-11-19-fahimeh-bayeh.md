---
speaker: Fahimeh Bayeh
title: The Category of Nominal Vector Spaces
date: 2026-11-19
---
How do you do linear algebra in symmetric spaces, including the infinite-atom case? Standard Hilbert spaces don't cut it: finite-dimensional ones are compact closed, but arbitrary infinite-dimensional ones aren't (the coevaluation sums over a basis and blows up). This talk introduces nominal vector spaces: linearize an orbit-finite nominal set, and the permutation symmetry is built in. The category NomVec(B,K) is a dagger compact closed category (tensor, duals, adjoints) yet it is pre-Hilbert: each object has a positive-definite inner product, but only over the finitely-supported, orbit-constant vectors; the infinite-support vectors (arising from the atom set A) sit in the completion, outside the category. The payoff: a rigorous home for symmetric linear algebra, with the scope kept exactly to the finitely-supported, orbit-constant part (and a bridge to quantum computing and symmetry-aware machine learning).

title: How to make a website (no AI needed)
---
Having a personal website is a good way to put yourself on the internet. Building a website is fun and easy. We will learn things such as:

- how to use `.css` files to easily customize your website
- how to use a static site generator for easy maintenance
- how to make sure your website is prioritized by search results
- how to make your (digital) dreams come true

Please bring your laptop to the seminar, as well as information you want to have on your website (resume, publications, presentations given, etc.).

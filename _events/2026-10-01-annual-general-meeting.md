It's time for the Mathematics and Statistics Graduate Student Society (MSGSS) Annual General Meeting and Elections! Join us for pizza, elections and board games.

We're calling nominations for:

- President
- Vice President External
- Vice President Internal
- Mathematics Masters Representative
- Mathematics PhD Representative
- Statistics Masters Representative
- Statistics PhD Representative

Any member can be nominated, and every grad student is already a member. Most nominations are self-nominations.

Questions? We'll explain each of these roles and recap the society's activities during the AGM, but you're welcome to ask us beforehand at [msgss@dal.ca](mailto:msgss@dal.ca).

Whether you're running for a position or just want free pizza and some games, we'd love to see you there!

# Travel Grant

## MSGSS travel grant

MSGSS ran a pilot travel grant in Summer 2026, which supported 2 students with $250 each for conference travel.

We are working to bring the grant back during the 2026/27 academic year. We'll announce it by email and on [Instagram]({{ site.data.society.instagram }}).

## Other travel funding

Graduate students in the department can also apply to the programs below. Each has its own rules about combining it with other funding, so check before applying. Amounts were last checked in October 2026.

### FGS Travel Grant

Dalhousie's Faculty of Graduate Studies gives **up to $500, once per degree**, to full-time students in thesis-based master's and PhD programs who are presenting their thesis research at a national or international meeting. Your supervisor signs the application, and you apply **at least one month before** the conference.

Students in the Faculty of Science can also apply for a one-time **supplement of up to $500** by ticking the box on the same form, for in-person conferences at least 100 km from Halifax.

[FGS Travel Grant details and application](https://www.dal.ca/faculty/gradstudies/finance-your-studies/scholarships-bursaries/travel-grant.html)

### AARMS Junior Researcher Travel Support

AARMS gives **up to $800 per fiscal year** to students supervised by math and stats faculty at member universities, including Dalhousie, for research-related travel. You apply **before the trip** by email, with a cost estimate and a note of support from your supervisor. The program opens April 1 each year and is first-come, first-served until the budget runs out. Funds are paid after the trip, so keep your receipts and boarding passes.

[AARMS travel support details](https://www.aarms.math.ca/programs/travel-support)

### DSU Member Grant

The Dalhousie Student Union gives DSU members **up to $100 per academic year** toward academic or professional conferences, including presenting research. Apply **within 30 days after the event**, in the same semester, with proof of expenses and enrolment.

[DSU grants and bursaries](https://www.dsu.ca/grants-bursaries)

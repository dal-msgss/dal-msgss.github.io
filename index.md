# Dalhousie Mathematics and Statistics Graduate Student Society

The Mathematics and Statistics Graduate Student Society (MSGSS) is the DSU society for all MSc and PhD students in mathematics and statistics at Dalhousie University. We organize a graduate student seminar, organize social events, and advocate for graduate students.

## Events

Meet the society 2026/27 [executive team](executive.html)! Here are some things we have planned for this year:

- **[Grad seminar](seminar/):** a weekly seminar where graduate students can share their research or any other findings in an informal, friendly setting. If you want to give a talk or coordinate a workshop, reach out at [msgss@dal.ca](mailto:msgss@dal.ca).
- **Writing days:** weekly co-working sessions at Chase 319 or at a nearby café.
- **Board game nights:** monthly social evenings that bring together students from across the department.
- **Career day:** an event with invited Dalhousie alumni and former postdocs now working in academia and industry, to help current students to plan for life after graduation. (Planned for Winter 2027.)
- **Travel grant:** we ran a pilot travel grant in Summer 2026, which supported 2 students with $250 each for conference travel. We are working to bring the grant back for this academic year.
- **Society maintenance:** one of our priority areas this year is working towards the long-term health of the society. We already updated this website and added [instructions](docs/instructions.html) for future executive teams, and we will change the society election month from October to March.
